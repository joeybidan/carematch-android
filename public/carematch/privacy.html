This policy covers CareMatch: Shift Arcade, operated by Joey Bidan Studios (Joey Bidan). It covers the standalone game and these support pages, not other apps hosted on the same domain.

1. Contact and your choices

For privacy, support or deletion requests, email joeybidanstudiosupport@gmail.com. You can play offline without an online account. Choose a nickname; never enter patient details, a real email, phone number or other sensitive information as your alias.

3. What we do not collect through the game

This version has no advertising, payment, analytics or tracking SDK. It does not ask for camera, microphone, contacts, precise location or health records. All people and care requests in gameplay are fictional. We do not sell personal data or use it for cross-app advertising.

4. Providers and disclosure

Supabase hosts the online game API and database; the configured database region is Seoul, South Korea. Netlify hosts these public pages. Gmail handles support email. Data may be processed outside your country. Public aliases and scores can be viewed, copied or shared by other players. Providers receive information needed to deliver their services. We may disclose information when required by law or needed to address fraud, abuse or security incidents.

Provider notices: Supabase privacy, Netlify privacy, Google privacy.

5. Retention

Online round tickets expire after 24 hours. Expired rounds without saved scores and CareMatch IP hashes older than 24 hours are removed by maintenance; cleanup runs hourly when scheduled and on API requests as a fallback. Saved scores and their associated rounds remain for the all-time leaderboard until you delete them or they are removed during moderation. Reviewed reports are removed 90 days after review; unresolved reports remain until reviewed. Alias restrictions remain until lifted, to prevent repeated abuse. Provider logs and backups follow the provider's retention settings rather than the live-game deletion schedule. Support correspondence is retained while needed to resolve your request and address follow-up, security or legal obligations.

6. Deletion and other privacy requests

Open Privacy & Support → Delete my game data in the game, review the confirmation, and confirm. With connectivity, this removes online rounds and scores linked to this installation and reports it submitted, then clears local scores, alias, blocks, pending uploads, cached rankings, rules acceptance and device key. Deletion ends your current game and cannot be undone.

Deleting app storage or uninstalling before using this feature can lose the key needed for automatic online deletion. Older app versions did not attach installation keys to scores. If your key is lost or your scores came from an older version, use the external deletion page or email us. Give the alias, approximate date and score so we can help verify the record. An alias alone is not proof of ownership; names are not reserved and may be shared. We do not automatically delete another person's records based only on a matching name.

Deleting your records does not delete scores from other installations, copies taken by other people, provider backup snapshots before they expire, or independent safety restrictions that remain necessary. We will explain any information that must be retained. You may also ask about access, correction or other rights available under applicable law.

7. Children and security

Online rankings are intended for players aged 13 and above and are not designed to collect children's personal information. If you believe a child supplied personal information, contact support. Connections use HTTPS. Database access is limited to the server, but no service can guarantee perfect security. Use a nickname and keep your installation key private.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when functionality or data practices change. The current version will be available here and in the game. Online play requires the current Player Rules; significant changes will be explained before renewed acceptance.
