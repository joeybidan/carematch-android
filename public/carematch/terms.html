CareMatch: Shift Arcade is a fictional puzzle game operated by Joey Bidan Studios (Joey Bidan). It does not arrange actual care, employ caregivers, provide medical advice or offer real-money rewards.

1. Accepting the rules

Before starting an online round or submitting a report, accept these Terms & Player Rules in the game. Online play is intended for players aged 13 or above; acceptance confirms you meet that requirement. Offline gameplay does not upload scores. If you do not agree, use offline play. Read the Privacy Policy for data practices. Deletion remains available without accepting new rules.

2. Respectful aliases and conduct

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behavior. Do not use aliases containing sexual content, profanity, slurs, hateful symbols, threats, bullying, harassment or discriminatory attacks. Do not impersonate people or organizations, publish another person's private information, or use real patient or caregiver records. Do not bypass filters with punctuation, repeated characters, look-alike letters or alternate spelling. Aliases support 1–12 English letters or numbers, plus spaces, dots, dashes and underscores, starting with a letter or number.

3. Fair play

Do not forge scores, manipulate requests, automate score farming, overload the service or attempt to access other players' private data. Scores are checked by replaying moves. A valid replay does not prove a human played it; suspected cheating can still be reviewed. Use reporting honestly and do not submit malicious or repeated false reports.

4. Rankings and blocking

Saved online aliases and scores are public. Rankings show the best score per alias; weekly rankings use Monday 12:00 AM Philippine time. Aliases are not accounts, reserved names or verified identities, so two installations using the same alias can share a ranking. You can block an alias to hide its scores on this device, and unblock it in Privacy & Support. Blocking hides every score using that alias and does not prevent someone from choosing a different alias.

5. Reporting and moderation

Tap Report beside a global ranking, choose a reason and send it. Reports are private and require a connection. A failure message means the report was not confirmed; retry or contact joeybidanstudiosupport@gmail.com. Blocking works locally without a connection.

The studio reviews reports and may restrict aliases, hide scores, remove records or lift a restriction after review. A report does not automatically ban someone. Report volume alone is not proof of wrongdoing. Filters can miss abusive variants or flag innocent names; choose another alias or contact support to request review. If a restriction appears mistaken, email support with the alias and details. Active human moderation is required while online rankings are offered.

6. Your data and requests

You can delete data linked to this installation in the game or request help through the Support & deletion page. We may need information to verify ownership, especially for old versions and shared aliases. Do not send passwords or secret device keys by email.

7. Game access and rights

The studio grants you permission to use the game for personal play. The service may be unavailable or changed for maintenance, security or rule updates. No leaderboard placement, prize or uninterrupted availability is guaranteed. You retain rights in information you submit and permit us to process and display your public alias and score to run the game, subject to the Privacy Policy and deletion options. Nothing here removes non-waivable consumer or privacy rights under applicable law.

8. Contact and updates

Support, privacy requests, safety reports and appeals: joeybidanstudiosupport@gmail.com. Updated rules will be published here and bundled into app updates. The current acceptance version is 2026-10-11.
