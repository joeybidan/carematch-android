Game support and player reports

Email us for bugs, offensive aliases, suspected cheating, privacy inquiries or moderation appeals. Include the app version, device model, what happened and a screenshot if useful. Do not include patient records or private credentials.

In the game, use Report beside a global leaderboard entry to submit a private safety report. Use Block to hide the alias immediately on your device. Reports require connectivity; a failure notice means you should retry or email us. The studio must review reports and take appropriate action.

Email support

Delete your game data in the app

  1. Keep your current installation and connect to the internet.
  2. Open Privacy & Support and tap Delete my game data.
  3. Read the confirmation, check the deletion box and tap Confirm deletion.
  4. Wait for the success message. If the request fails, keep the app installed and retry.

This deletes online rounds and scores linked to this installation and reports it submitted, then clears local saved scores, alias, pending uploads, blocks, rules acceptance, cached rankings and the private device key. It also ends your current shift. It does not delete scores from other installations. No login or account creation is required.

Request deletion without the app

If you uninstalled, lost the device key, used an older version or need help with other personal information, email the address below with the subject CareMatch data deletion. Include your arcade alias, approximate play dates and scores, and whether you still have the app. Do not email your secret device key. We will help verify the relevant records and explain any verification steps before removing them.

Request data deletion by email

Aliases are shared, unverified names. A name alone does not authorize deletion of another person's records. Old app versions did not attach private installation keys; these records may need manual verification.

Data retained after a request

Necessary independent safety restrictions may remain until lifted. Provider logs and backup snapshots expire according to the provider's retention settings. Public copies made by others are outside our control. We will explain any data we cannot immediately remove and why. See the Privacy Policy for all retention details.
